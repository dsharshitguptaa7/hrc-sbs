THE FOUR MAJOR FIELDS

Foundational Disciplines

01

Representation Theory & Lie Algebras

Study of infinite-dimensional representations of real reductive groups, discrete series, and Harish-Chandra modules. The framework provides the structural algebraic language for symmetries in modern quantum mechanics.

Core Themes: Semisimple Lie Groups · Characters · Subquotient Theorem
02

Harmonic Analysis on Symmetric Spaces

Non-commutative Fourier analysis, invariant differential operators, and the Plancherel formula. Decomposition of \(L^2(G)\) and generalized spherical functions over Riemannian symmetric spaces.

Core Themes: Cusp Forms · Eisenstein Series · Harish-Chandra Schwartz Space
03

Theoretical & Mathematical Physics

Relativistic wave equations, Dirac spinors, quantum symmetries, gauge field theories, and topological phases of matter investigated at the interface of mathematics and physics.

Core Themes: Relativistic Dynamics · Quantum Symmetries · Field Theory
04

Automorphic Forms & The Langlands Program

Deep interconnections between harmonic analysis, representation theory, and arithmetic geometry. Harish-Chandra's work constitutes the foundational analytic cornerstone of modern Langlands conjectures.

Core Themes: L-Functions · Arthur-Selberg Trace Formula · Global Symmetries
