ABOUT THE INSTITUTION
HARISH-CHANDRA RESEARCH CENTRE

An Academic Home for Rigorous Thought

Constituted at the School of Basic Sciences, Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University, Kanpur, in academic partnership with the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur.

SCHOLARLY GENESIS

Conceived in the Birthplace of Harish-Chandra

Dr. Harish-Chandra was born on October 11, 1923, in Kanpur. His transcendent path in theoretical physics and pure mathematics revolutionized harmonic analysis and infinite-dimensional representation theory worldwide.

To immortalize his intellectual courage and create an active center of learning in the city where his journey began, Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University established the Harish-Chandra Research Centre under the aegis of the School of Basic Sciences.

Through a sustained partnership with IIT Kanpur, the Centre bridges advanced theoretical inquiry with university education, offering scholars access to joint colloquia, archival literature, and collaborative scientific discourse.

“The purpose of the Centre is not merely to memorialize past triumph, but to cultivate a living standard of uncompromising curiosity.”

FOUR PILLARS OF EXCELLENCE

How the Centre Operates

01

Pure Research & Formulation

Supporting faculty, visiting mathematicians, and research fellows in pursuing deep structural questions in algebra, geometry, and fundamental quantum mechanics.

02

Scholarly Dialogue & Colloquia

Conducting the prestigious Annual Harish-Chandra Lecture Series and inter-departmental roundtables uniting scholars from CSJMU, IIT Kanpur, and abroad.

03

Pedagogical Mentorship

Nurturing undergraduate and postgraduate scholars at CSJMU through advanced seminars, reading groups in representation theory, and research training.

04

Living Archival Heritage

Preserving and exhibiting Dr. Harish-Chandra's letters, papers, books, and international impact through the Digital Legacy 2026 digital exhibition platform.
