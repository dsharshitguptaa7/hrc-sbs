EXHIBITION TRACK 11 / 12
CONTEMPORARY CONTINUITY

Then & Now

Connecting historical 20th-century mathematical breakthroughs with 21st-century education, quantum physics, computing, and society.

THEN

The 1950s: Abstract Representations

Harish-Chandra worked in isolation on infinite-dimensional unitary representations of non-compact Lie groups, considered by contemporaries as impossibly abstract mathematics.

NOW

Geometric Deep Learning & Symmetries

Modern machine learning incorporates group-equivariant neural networks and Lie group invariants to process 3D molecular structures and medical imaging.

THEN

The 1960s: Cusp Forms & Trace Formulas

Establishing the spectral decomposition of \(L^2(G/\Gamma)\) for arithmetic subgroups, leading into the foundational conjectures of Robert Langlands.

NOW

Quantum Field Theory & Duality

The Geometric Langlands Program and S-duality in supersymmetric gauge theories now utilize Harish-Chandra modules as non-perturbative topological tools.
