HARISH-CHANDRA
DIGITAL LEGACY 2026

Twelve ways to discover a legacy.

A student web experience and digital exhibition celebrating the journey, knowledge, contribution, and inspiration of Harish-Chandra. Each track represents a specialized curatorial dimension — spanning interactive biography, mathematical research, quizzes, maps, and archival preservation.

The 12 Digital Exhibition Tracks
