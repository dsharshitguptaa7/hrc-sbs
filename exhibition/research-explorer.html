EXHIBITION TRACK 05 / 12
MATHEMATICAL TAXONOMY

Research Explorer

Interactive cataloguing of Harish-Chandra's work across Lie algebras, harmonic analysis, and representation theory.

CATEGORY A

Representations of Semisimple Lie Groups

Classification of irreducible representations on Banach and Hilbert spaces; the subquotient theorem; character formulas.

CATEGORY B

Non-Commutative Harmonic Analysis

The explicit Plancherel formula for real reductive groups; spherical transform; Harish-Chandra Schwartz spaces.

CATEGORY C

Invariant Eigendistributions

Harish-Chandra's regularity theorem for characters; proving that distributions satisfying Laplace-type systems are locally \(L^1\) functions.

CATEGORY D

Automorphic Forms & Eisenstein Series

General theory of Eisenstein series and cusp forms on reductive groups, setting the foundational machinery for modern Langlands theory.
