Legacy Map

Tracing the intellectual institutions, mentors, and global centres shaped by Harish-Chandra.

GLOBAL GEOGRAPHY OF AN INTELLECTUAL ODYSSEY
01

Kanpur

1923 · Birthplace & Formative Years

Home, foundational schooling, and roots commemorated by HRC CSJMU today.

02

Allahabad

1941–43 · University of Allahabad

M.Sc. Physics under Prof. K. S. Krishnan, FRS; introduction to modern quantum physics.

03

Bangalore

1943–45 · IISc Bangalore

Research with Homi Bhabha on relativistic wave equations and cosmic radiation.

04

Cambridge

1945–47 · Cambridge University

Gonville & Caius College; Ph.D. under Paul Dirac; interaction with Wolfgang Pauli.

05

Princeton

1947–49 & 1963–83 · IAS Princeton

IBM von Neumann Chair; creation of non-commutative harmonic analysis.
