HISTORICAL IMPACT

Legacy of Harish-Chandra

Fundamental discoveries, structural achievements, and long-term impact on global mathematics and physics.

01

Infinite-Dimensional Representations

Prior to Harish-Chandra, representations of non-compact groups were largely uncharted. He classified unitary representations and established the existence of discrete series representations for semisimple Lie groups with compact Cartan subgroups.

02

Harish-Chandra Characters & The Plancherel Formula

Proved that the characters of infinite-dimensional representations are locally integrable functions on the group, analytical on the regular set, solving one of the deepest challenges in non-commutative harmonic analysis.

03

The Foundation of the Langlands Program

Harish-Chandra's theory of cusp forms and Eisenstein series provided Robert Langlands with the exact analytical apparatus needed to formulate the global Langlands correspondences linking number theory with automorphic representations.
