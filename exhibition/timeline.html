CHRONOLOGICAL CURATION

The Harish-Chandra Timeline

Major historical turning points, pivotal decisions, breakthroughs, and international appointments.

1923

Born in Kanpur

October 11, 1923: Born in Kanpur, United Provinces, India.

1943

University of Allahabad Master of Science

First rank in M.Sc. Physics; advised by Prof. K. S. Krishnan, FRS.

1947

Cambridge University Ph.D. under Dirac

Conferred doctorate; travels to IAS Princeton as Dirac's assistant.

1950 – 1963

Columbia University & Mathematical Foundation

Formulation of characters of semisimple Lie groups and Plancherel formula.

1963

Appointed IBM von Neumann Professor at IAS Princeton

Permanent member and chair at the world’s foremost institute of advanced research.

1973

Elected Fellow of the Royal Society (FRS)

Conferred highest scientific fellowship of the United Kingdom.
